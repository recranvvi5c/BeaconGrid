# Code of Conduct

BeaconGrid is a small, focused project built on a simple promise: reviewers should
be able to trust a replay dossier, and contributors should be able to trust each
other. The same standards of clarity and good faith we hold our code to, we hold
our community to.

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in BeaconGrid a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity and expression, sexual
orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age,
religion, or nationality.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith. A confusing bug report or an imperfect pull request is an
  opportunity to help, not to belittle.
- Keep technical disagreement technical. Argue the scoring model, the divergence
  classification, or the runtime-parity tradeoff — never the person.
- Be precise. Because BeaconGrid values deterministic, traceable behavior, we
  expect discussion to cite evidence: a failing dossier, a diff, a reproduction.
- Respect the project's scope. "Out of scope" is a legitimate, respectful answer,
  not a dismissal.
- Give credit and accept correction gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, and reviews — and whenever an individual is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that
violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior confidentially to the maintainers at
**conduct@beacongrid.dev**. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of anyone
who reports an incident.
